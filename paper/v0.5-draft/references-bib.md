Each entry: full citation + DOI (or `[DOI: pending]` where verification was not possible without external lookup) + arXiv / URL when available + one-sentence relevance note tying the entry to the specific section that uses it.

**Honesty note.** Entries with `[DOI: pending]` are real publications that could not be programmatically resolved to a DOI from inside this repository. They are flagged for human verification before final submission. No citation is fabricated.
